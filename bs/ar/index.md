## How to Use

1. **Choose your language version** - Select English or Chinese
2. **View online** - Browse the reading plan directly on the website
3. **Download Excel** - Get the original Excel file for offline use or printing
4. **Follow the schedule** - Each week includes 6 reading days and 1 review day
5. **Stay consistent** - Check off completed readings as you progress

---

## Reading Tips

- 📖 Set a regular time each day for Bible reading
- ✍️ Keep a journal to note insights and reflections
- 🙏 Begin each session with prayer
- 📝 Mark difficult passages to study later
- 👥 Consider joining a reading group for accountability
