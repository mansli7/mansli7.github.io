## How to Use Study Questions

1. **📖 Read the passage** - Start by reading the biblical text carefully
2. **🤔 Reflect individually** - Think through each question on your own
3. **✍️ Write your thoughts** - Journal your insights and applications
4. **👥 Discuss in groups** - Share and learn from others' perspectives
5. **🙏 Pray for wisdom** - Ask God to reveal truth through His Word

---

## Question Types

Our study guides include:
- 🔍 **Observation** - What does the text say?
- 💭 **Interpretation** - What does it mean?
- 🎯 **Application** - How does it apply to my life?
- 🌐 **Connection** - How does it relate to other Scripture?
- 🙏 **Response** - How should I respond to God?
