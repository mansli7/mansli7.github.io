Leviticus 1-4

---


1. Why are burnt offerings to be presented?

2. What kinds of livestock or birds can be used for a burnt offering?

3. What must the grain offering be seasoned with, and what must not be included?

4. How do the animals for the fellowship offering differ from those for the burnt offering?

5. When someone sins unintentionally by doing something the LORD commanded not to be done, what offering is required?

---


Leviticus 5-8

---


1. Is failing to testify truthfully a sin?

2. If so, what offering is to be brought to seek forgiveness?

3. Is lying a sin?

4. Is the fire on the altar allowed to go out?

5. Can Aaron and his sons eat what is left of the grain offering?

6. What about the grain offering presented for the priests themselves?

7. Which two offerings are governed by the same law?

8. If the offering is for thanksgiving, what kind of offering is brought?

9. When sacrifices are offered to ordain Aaron and his sons as priests, what is burned outside the camp?

---


Leviticus 9-12

---


1. When Aaron offered the sin offering, burnt offering, and fellowship offering and the glory of the LORD appeared to all the people, in what year, month, and day after Israel came out of Egypt did this occur?

2. Why were Nadab and Abihu put to death?

3. Were the Israelites allowed to eat pork?

4. On which day after birth was a boy to be circumcised?

---


Leviticus 13-16

---


1. If a person was unclean, where was he to live?

2. Who was responsible for determining whether a person or a garment was clean or unclean?

3. Did a person seeking cleansing from uncleanness need to bring offerings?

4. Could a house be affected by a defiling skin disease?

5. On which day was the Day of Atonement?

---


Leviticus 17-20

---


1. Was it permitted to eat blood?

2. Can blood atone for sin?

3. Were the Israelites allowed to follow the practices of the Egyptians or the Canaanites?

4. If not, how were they to live?

5. Which verse from Leviticus 18 is quoted in the book of Galatians?

6. Was it permitted to show favoritism to the poor?

7. Who quoted “Love your neighbor as yourself” in the New Testament?

8. If an Israelite sacrificed his children to Molek, what should be done?

9. If an Israelite sacrifices their children to Molek, what should the people of the land do?

---


Leviticus 21-24

---


1. Who is considered the chief among the people?

2. Note: You can easy to find answer in KJV. In the NIV, the chief idea is implied rather than stated. What is referred to as the food of God?

3. Can a descendant of Aaron who has a defiling skin disease eat the sacred offerings?

4. When is the Festival of Weeks (Pentecost) celebrated?

5. On which day is a memorial proclaimed with trumpet blasts?

6. When is the Festival of Tabernacles celebrated?

7. How is a person who blasphemes the name of the LORD to be punished?

---


Leviticus 25-27

---


1. How often is the Year of Jubilee to be celebrated?

2. Are Israelites allowed to charge interest when lending to one another?

3. If the Israelites violate the LORD’s covenant, will the LORD forget the covenant He has made with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob?

4. How long a period of time does the book of Leviticus cover?
