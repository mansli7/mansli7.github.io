Jonah 1-4

Where did the LORD send Jonah to go, and what was he told to do? Instead, where did Jonah board a ship to flee to, and from whom was he trying to escape?

When the violent storm and raging sea came upon the ship, what did the sailors do to save themselves, and what was Jonah doing? How was the raging sea finally calmed?

How many days and nights was Jonah in the belly of the fish? Who in the New Testament refers to the sign of Jonah?

From where did Jonah call for help? What barred him in forever? From where did the LORD bring his life up, and from whom does salvation come?

What message did Jonah proclaim in Nineveh, and was it fulfilled? Why or why not?

Why did Jonah become displeased and angry, and even ask to die?

What did the LORD God provide that made Jonah greatly rejoice, and what did He later provide that caused Jonah again to become angry and ask to die?

What was Jonah concerned about, and what was the LORD concerned about?
