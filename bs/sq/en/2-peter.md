2 Peter 1-3

1. “If you possess these qualities in increasing measure, they will keep you from being ineffective and unproductive in your knowledge of our Lord Jesus Christ.” What are “these qualities”?

2. What happens to those who do not have these qualities?

3. “I think it is right to refresh your memory as long as I live in the tent of this body.” What does “this tent” mean?

4. When and where did Peter personally hear the voice saying, “This is my Son, whom I love; with him I am well pleased”?

5. What did he see with his own eyes?

6. Where did the voice come from?

7. Who is the beloved Son?

8. In the first chapter of 2 Peter, what does Peter say is “above all”?

9. When the flood came on the ungodly world, why did God protect Noah and the other seven members of his family?

10. When God condemned the cities of Sodom and Gomorrah by burning them to ashes, why did he rescue Lot?

11. What kind of way did Balaam son of Bezer follow?

12. “A dog returns to its vomit” and “A washed pig returns to her wallowing in the mud.” Who are the people to whom these sayings apply?

13. The Lord is not slow in keeping his promise, as some understand slowness. Why is he patient instead?

14. On that day, the heavens will be destroyed by fire and the elements will melt in the heat. But in keeping with his promise, what are we looking forward to?

15. Whose day is “that day”?
