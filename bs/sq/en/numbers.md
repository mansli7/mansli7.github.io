Numbers 1-4

---


1. How did the LORD instruct Moses to count the Israelite army?

2. How many tribes were counted?

3. What was the total number of people?

4. Were the Levites included, and why or why not?

5. Where was the Levite camp located?

6. How were the Levites counted?

7. How many were there?

8. Which tribe took the place of the firstborn of Israel?

9. How many Levites performed the carrying work in the tabernacle,that is,the tent of meeting?

---


Numbers 5-8

---


1. Why must the unclean go outside the camp?

2. What does Nazirite mean?

3. How should Aaron and his sons bless the Israelites?

4. On the days the altar is anointed, how many days do the leaders offer their offerings?

5. Are the offerings the same each day?

6. Which tribe is set apart from the Israelites for the LORD?

---


Numbers 9-12

---


1. If someone is unclean due to contact with a dead body and cannot celebrate the Passover, is there other day to celebrate it?

2. Does the Israelites’ journey or camping relate to the cloud over the tabernacle?

3. On what day did the Israelites leave the Desert of Sinai?

4. What does Moses say whenever the ark sets out?

5. What does he say whenever it comes to rest?

6. What does Kibroth Hattaavah mean?

7. Who began to talk against Moses?

8. Who is described as extremely humble?

---


Numbers 13-16

---


1. From where did the twelve men set out to explore the land of Canaan?

2. How many days did they spend exploring?

3. How many spread a bad report?

4. How did the whole congregation react after hearing the report?

5. For how many years will the Israelites wander in the wilderness?

6. Whose body will lie in the wilderness?

7. Who will be allowed to enter the land of Canaan?

8. What do you learn from Numbers 14:39-45?

9. Do foreigner and native-born have the same laws and regulations?

10. What position did Korah and his followers try to get?

11. How did they die?

12. How was the plague stopped?

---


Numbers 17-20

---


1. Whose staff will sprout?

2. Which firstborn must be redeemed?

3. Which firstborn cannot be redeemed?

4. What fraction of Israel’s produce is given to the LORD as a wave offering?

5. What is the ashes of the red heifer used for?

6. Where did Miriam die?

7. What did Moses and Aaron do wrong at the waters of Meribah?

8. Where did Aaron die?

---


Numbers 21-24

---


1. What did Moses make and put it up on a pole?

2. Whoever was bitten by a snake would live by looking at it?

3. In the New Testament, who said he would be lifted up in the same way so that everyone who believes in him may have eternal life?

4. Why did the king of Moab summon Balaam, and why did he refuse the first time but go the second time?

5. Who caused Balaam’s donkey to speak?

6. The king of Moab summoned Balaam to curse Israel, but he blessed Israel instead. From whom did this blessing come?

---


Numbers 25-28

---


1. In Numbers 25, what caused the plague?

2. What caused the plague to stop?

3. On the plains of Moab by the Jordan opposite Jericho, how many Israelites were counted?

4. Compared to the count in the wilderness of Sinai, which tribe increased the most?

5. Which tribe decreased the most?

6. Who were counted in both censuses?

7. If a man dies without a son, to whom does his inheritance go?

8. Why was Moses not allowed to enter Canaan?

9. In Numbers 28, for what occasions did God instruct Moses to present the offerings?

---


Numbers 29-32

---


1. In Numbers 29, for what occasions did God instruct Moses to present the offerings?

2. If a man makes a vow to the LORD or takes an oath to bind himself by a pledge, can he break his word?

3. Why did they fight against the Midianites?

4. Why was Balaam killed?

5. How is Balaam described in the New Testament?

6. Do those who did not go to war share in the spoils?

7. Do the Levites have a share?

8. Which tribes asked for the land east of the Jordan as their inheritance?

---


Numbers 33-36

---


1. Think again: when the king of Moab summoned Balaam, why did Balaam go the second time?

2. How many places did the Israelites camp at from leaving Egypt until entering Canaan?

3. After entering Canaan, what happens if the inhabitants are not driven out?

4. Into how many tribes was the land of Canaan divided as inheritance?

5. How many cities did the Levites receive?

6. How many of these were cities of refuge?

7. What is the purpose of the cities of refuge?

8. Can a daughter who receives an inheritance in one of the Israelite tribes marry someone from another tribe?
