# 1 Samuel Study Questions

---


1 Samuel 1–4

---


1. What did Hannah say in her distress, crying out and praying to the LORD, and what vow did she make?

2. Why did Hannah name her son Samuel?

3. Why were Eli’s two sons guilty of such serious sin in the sight of the LORD?

4. Did Eli restrain them?

5. Could sacrifices atone for the sins of Eli’s house?

6. Which verses in 1 Samuel 3 speak about “revelation”?

7. Why was the Ark of the Covenant captured?

---


1 Samuel 5–8

---


1. Why did the Philistines return the Ark of God to Israel?

2. Why did the LORD strike down seventy men of Beth Shemesh?

3. What stone did Samuel set up between Mizpah and Shen, what did he name it, and what did he say?

4. Israel wanted to appoint a king to govern them because they had rejected whom and did not want whom as their king?

---


1 Samuel 9–12

---


1. Who brought Saul from the territory of Benjamin to Samuel?

2. How was Saul transformed into a new man?

3. When casting lots to choose Saul as king, where did he hide?

4. Where did the people make Saul king before the LORD?

5. In seeking to appoint a king, against whom did Israel commit a great sin?

---


1 Samuel 13–16

---


1. In which year of Saul’s reign did he act foolishly and disobey whose command, and would his throne endure?

2. Was Saul’s oath in 1 Samuel 14 good?

3. Both involved killing enemies—was Joshua’s prayer in chapter 10 better?

4. Obedience is better than what, and submission is better than what?

5. The sin of rebellion is equivalent to what?

6. The sin of stubbornness is equal to what?

7. Who rejected the LORD’s command, and whom did the LORD reject as king?

8. Whom did Samuel anoint as king of Israel in Bethlehem, whose spirit was greatly empowered by the LORD, and from whom did the LORD’s spirit depart?

---


1 Samuel 17–20

---


1. Goliath challenged Israel for forty days; did anyone face him?

2. What did Goliath rely on to attack David, and what did David rely on to attack Goliath?

3. After David killed Goliath, how did Jonathan treat David, and how did Saul treat David?

4. Whom should we learn from?

5. Saul demanded a hundred Philistine foreskins as a bride price—was he truly willing for David to marry his daughter?

6. After David defeated the Philistines greatly, did Saul want to reward him or kill him?

7. Saul sought to kill David, but who allied with David’s house?

---


1 Samuel 21–24

---


1. Who is mentioned in the New Testament as entering the house of God and eating the consecrated bread?

2. During his flight, did David go to Philistine or Moabite territory?

3. Who commanded his men to kill Ahimelek, the priest of the LORD?

4. Whom did David consult about saving Keilah, and whom did he consult about leaving Keilah to escape Saul?

5. In the cave, David had the opportunity to kill Saul but did not—why?

6. Did Saul know that David would become king of Israel?

---


1 Samuel 25–28

---


1. David sent his servant to see Nabal—if you were in the same situation, would you respond like Nabal or like Abigail to David?

2. In Saul’s camp, David again had the chance to kill Saul but did not—why?

3. Did Saul know he was guilty?

4. Did David tell Achish that he had invaded Judah—was this true?

5. When Saul inquired of the LORD, did the LORD answer him?

6. Who had cut off the mediums and later sought a medium for inquiry?

---


1 Samuel 29–31

---


1. The Philistines gathered at Aphek to fight Israel; did the Philistine commanders allow David to go out with them?

2. Whom did David consult about whether he could pursue the Amalekites?

3. From the spoils David took from the Amalekites, how much did the fighting men and the guards each receive?

4. Saul and his three sons died in battle against whom?

5. Who killed Saul?
