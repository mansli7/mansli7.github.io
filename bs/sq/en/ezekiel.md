# Ezekiel Study Questions

---

Ezekiel 1—4

1. When and where did the word of the LORD come to Ezekiel, and the hand of the LORD was on him?

2. Where was Ezekiel sent?

3. What did Ezekiel eat that tasted as sweet as honey in his mouth?

4. Ezekiel was appointed as a watchman for the people of Israel; what is his responsibility?

5. How many days did Ezekiel lie on his left side and right side, and what did each bear?

---

Ezekiel 5—8

1. What did the LORD have Ezekiel prophesy by dividing his hair into three parts?

2. How many times does Ezekiel 6 say, "you (or they) will know that I am the LORD"?

3. In Ezekiel 7, how many times is the word "end" mentioned?

4. In Ezekiel 8, how many detestable things did Ezekiel see?

---

Ezekiel 9—12

1. In Ezekiel 9, the LORD commanded the killing of Jerusalem's inhabitants, but spared what kind of people?

2. Do you remember in which book the glory of the LORD first filled the temple?

3. In Ezekiel 10, what departed from the temple?

4. Where will the LORD be a sanctuary for the Israelites for a little while?

5. What will the LORD remove from them, and what will He give them?

6. In Ezekiel 12, whom does Ezekiel's acted prophecy concern?

7. Which is the LORD's statement: "The days go by and every vision comes to nothing" or "The days are near when every vision will be fulfilled"?

---

Ezekiel 13—16

1. Are those who prophesy out of their own imagination truly prophets of the LORD?

2. In Ezekiel 13, how many times does the LORD say, "you shall know that I am the LORD"?

3. Can the righteousness of Noah, Daniel, and Job save their own sons or daughters?

4. In Ezekiel 15, whom does the burned vine represent?

5. Compared with Judah, Sodom, and Samaria, who committed the most detestable things?

---

Ezekiel 17—20

1. Who is the "he" who is to be punished?

2. If a wicked person turns from all sins and keeps God's statutes, will he live?

3. Will his former offenses be remembered?

4. If a righteous person turns from their righteousness and commits sin, will he live?

5. Will his former righteousness be remembered?

6. Who is the young lion taken with hooks to Egypt, and who is the young trapped lion taken to Babylon?

7. In Ezekiel 20, how many times does the LORD intend to pour out His wrath on Israel, and why is it withheld?

8. Why does the Lord GOD swear by His life that He will not be inquired by Israel?

---

Ezekiel 21—24

1. Two roads are appointed for the sword of the king of Babylon—where does each road lead?

2. As silver is melted in a furnace, who will be melted in Jerusalem?

3. Who are Aholah and Aholibah, and will they suffer the penalty for their lewdness?

4. When did the wife who was the delight of Ezekiel's eyes die, and what was this a sign of?

---

Ezekiel 25—28

1. Which four nations does the LORD pronounce judgment on in Ezekiel 25?

2. Which city will become a bare rock, a place for spreading nets, and never to be rebuilt?

3. Who claimed to be perfect in beauty but became desolate in the sea?

4. Who is a mere mortal and not a god, though you think you are as wise as a god?

5. Is there a difference between the "prince of Tyre" and the "king of Tyre" in Ezekiel 28?

---

Ezekiel 29—32

1. Who said, "The Nile belongs to me; I made it for myself"?

2. Which land will be desolate for forty years?

3. At the date given in Ezekiel 30:20, had Jerusalem already fallen?

4. Is Ezekiel 31 about Assyria, or about Pharaoh and Egypt?

5. What does "there" refer to in Ezekiel 32:22, 24, 26, 29, and 30?

---

Ezekiel 33—36

1. What is Ezekiel's responsibility as a watchman?

2. Who said, "I myself will shepherd my sheep"?

3. Who is meant by "my servant David"?

4. Who harbored an ancient hostility and delivered Israel over to the sword?

5. Who are the "they" in "they will soon come home"?

---

Ezekiel 37—40

1. Who will open Israel's graves, raise them to life, and bring them back to the land?

2. What does joining the two sticks into one symbolize?

3. Who will attack Israel in the latter days, and from where do they come?

4. How will the LORD judge them, and where will they be buried?

5. For whom are the north and south chambers prepared, and what are their duties?

---

Ezekiel 41—44

1. What is the inner sanctuary called, and what are its dimensions?

2. May priests go directly from the holy place to the outer court?

3. Through which gate did the glory of the LORD enter the temple, and may that gate be opened again?

4. Which Levites are excluded from the priesthood, and whose descendants may serve?

---

Ezekiel 45—48

1. Which festivals are mentioned in Ezekiel 45:18–25?

2. Through which gate do the people of the land enter to worship, and through which gate do they leave?

3. Which direction does the temple face?

4. From where does the water flow, where does it flow to, and what does this water accomplish?

5. How many gates does the city have, what are their names, and what is the city called?
