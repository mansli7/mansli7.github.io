2 Thessalonians 1-3

Why did Paul and his companions always thank God for the church in Thessalonica?

How many times does 2 Thessalonians mention “pay back” or “punishment”?

Who will pay back whom, and when will this happen?

At the same time, who will have peace, and who will receive glory?

Before the day of the Lord comes, what must happen?

Who will be revealed?

Whom will he oppose, whom will he exalt himself over, where will he take his seat, and what will he claim to be?

Then by whom will he be overthrown and destroyed?

Why should Paul and his companions always thank God for the church in Thessalonica?

What did Paul ask the Thessalonians to pray for them?

What kind of people should believers keep away from?

For example, what kind of people?
