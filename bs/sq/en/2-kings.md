2 Kings 1–4

---


1. Why would Ahaziah not recover from the bed he lay on, and why was he certain to die?

2. Why was the third captain with his fifty men not consumed by the fire sent from heaven?

3. By whom, in what manner was Elijah taken, and to where?

4. Before Elijah was taken, what request did Elisha made of him?

5. What miracles did Elisha perform in these four chapters?

---


2 Kings 5–8

---


1. Who advised Naaman of Aram to go to Samaria to be healed of leprosy?

2. Did the king of Israel have a way to heal him?

3. What was the method of healing provided by the man of God, Elisha?

4. Did Naaman accept it at first?

5. Later, after whose advice did he follow Elisha’s instructions, and was he cleansed?

6. When Naaman returned to the man of God, what did he now know, what did he request Elisha to give him, what did he intend to use it for, and what did he ask the LORD to forgive him for?

7. During Elisha’s time, were any lepers in Israel cleansed?

8. Elisha did not accept Naaman’s gift. Who, however, pursued Naaman to demand a gift, and as a result, he and his descendants were afflicted with Naaman’s leprosy?

9. When Elisha was besieged in Dothan by the Aramean army, what did he say to his servant who did not know what to do?

10. Where in the Bible was it prophesied that they would eat their own children, and whose fault was this disaster?

11. Who would see it with their own eyes but not eat it, and why?

12. Upon whom did Elisha fix his gaze and cry, and why?

13. Who married Ahab’s daughter and did evil in the eyes of the LORD?

---


2 Kings 9–12

---


1. Whom did Elisha’s disciples anoint as king of Israel, and what did the LORD commission this king to do?

2. Which king did Jehu kill along with King Jehoram of Israel?

3. How was Jezebel killed, what happened to Ahab’s seventy sons, was all of Ahab’s house destroyed, and was the LORD’s word spoken through Elijah fulfilled?

4. Were the Baal worshippers killed?

5. Did Jehu fully obey the law of the LORD?

6. How was the northern kingdom of Israel reduced?

7. Who overthrew the royal family of Judah, reigned for six years, and whose daughter was she?

8. Who saved Joash, the son of Ahaziah, and where was he hidden?

9. On whom did Jehoiada place a crown, to him did he give the book of the law, and whom did he anointed as king?

10. Who made the king and the people enter into a covenant with the LORD, making them the LORD’s people, and also made a covenant between the king and the people?

11. How old was Joash when he became king, how many years did he reign, and did he do what was right in the eyes of the LORD?

---


2 Kings 13–16

---


1. King Hazael of Aram repeatedly oppressed Israel. Do you remember the prophecy concerning his oppression?

2. Why was Elisha angry with Joash?

3. King Amaziah of Judah killed the servants who had murdered his father but did not kill their children. Why, and in which book of the law is this commanded?

4. Whom did the LORD provide as a savior for Israel to deliver them from the hands of the Arameans, and why?

5. How many generations would Jehu’s descendants sit on Israel’s throne, and was the LORD’s word fulfilled?

6. During King Pekah’s reign, who captured what regions and deported their inhabitants to Assyria?

7. Which king of Judah reigned when the Aramean king and the king of Israel came up to attack Jerusalem?

8. Which king of Judah worshiped the idols of Damascus?

---


2 Kings 17–20

---


1. Who was the last king of Israel, and how many years did he reign?

2. Who captured Samaria and deported the Israelites to Assyria?

3. Why were Israel exiled — whom did they sin against, whom did they disobey, whose anger did they provoke, in whom did they not believe, whose statutes and what covenant did they reject, whose commandments did they forsake, and in whose eyes did they do evil?

4. Which peoples were resettled in Israel, and what else did they worship alongside their fear of the LORD?

5. In which year of Hezekiah’s reign was Israel exiled to Assyria?

6. When King Sennacherib of Assyria sent envoys to mock the LORD, how many were killed by the LORD’s angel in the Assyrian camp, and where did Sennacherib die?

7. Did Hezekiah ask for the shadow to move forward or backward, and do you remember which book records the sun standing still for a day?

8. After the Babylonian envoys departed, what did Isaiah say to Hezekiah?

---


2 Kings 21–25

---


1. Manasseh did evil in the eyes of the LORD even more than the nations that the LORD had destroyed before Israel. What did he build in the courts of the LORD’s temple, and what did he set up inside the temple?

2. Upon whom the LORD said He would bring disaster, with what measuring line and plumb line would be stretched over which place, what would be wiped out, and whom would He reject, and why?

3. When High Priest Hilkiah found the Book of the Law in the temple, was it the first time he and King Josiah learned its contents?

4. How did the king react in his heart?

5. Before whom did he humble himself, what did he tear, and to whom did he cry?

6. What did the LORD grant him?

7. Which book instructs the king to make a copy of the law for himself?

8. Do you remember what the LORD told the man of God to say to Jeroboam, and was it fulfilled?

9. From when until Josiah’s eighteenth year, was there truly no one like him who observed the Passover to the LORD?

10. Before and after Josiah, was there any king who turned to the LORD with all his heart, soul, and strength like him?

11. In which year of the Babylonian king’s reign did this happen — what did he carry away, what did he destroy, and just as had been foretold by whom?

12. Who was the last king of Judah, in which year of his reign was Jerusalem captured?

13. In what year, month, and day of King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon was the LORD’s temple burned with fire?
