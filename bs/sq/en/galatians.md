Galatians 1-3

1. What did the Galatian church turn away from, and what did they turn to?

2. Who should be under God’s curse?

3. Was the gospel Paul preached one of human origin? Why?

4. Whom did Paul oppose to his face in Antioch? Why?

5. “I have been crucified with Christ and I no longer live, but Christ lives in me; and the life I now live in the body, I live by faith in the Son of God, who loved me and gave himself for me.” What kind of person is the “I” described here?

6. What are the sources of “Abraham believed God, and it was credited to him as righteousness” and “All nations will be blessed through you”?

7. Why did Paul quote these two passages here?

8. Where are “All who rely on the works of the law are under a curse,” “The righteous will live by faith,” “The person who does these things will live by them,” and “Cursed is everyone who is hung on a pole” found?

9. Why did Paul quote these passages here?

10. The promise was spoken to Abraham and to his offspring: “to your seed.” Where is this found in the Old Testament?

11. Who is this “seed”?

---

Galatians 4-6

1. When the set time had fully come, whom did God send, born of a woman and born under what?

2. What did he come to redeem, and what were we to receive?

3. “These women represent two covenants.” Who are the two women, and what are the two covenants?

4. What is the source of “Rejoice, you childless woman, you who never bore a child; shout for joy and cry aloud, you who were never in labor; because more are the children of the desolate woman than of her who has a husband”?

5. What is the source of “Get rid of the slave woman and her son, for the slave woman’s son will never share in the inheritance with the free woman’s son”?

6. Who were “the slave woman’s son” and “the free woman’s son” there?

7. Who do they represent in Galatians?

8. What command sums up the whole law?

9. What are the acts of the flesh that are obvious?

10. Can those who live like this inherit the kingdom of God?

11. What is the fruit of the Spirit?

12. Is there any law against such things?

13. Why did Paul say that he would never boast except in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ?
