Matthew 1-3

1. How many sets of fourteen generations are there in the genealogy of Jesus Christ, and how many generations are there in total?

2. How many women are included?

3. How did Mary become pregnant, and why was the son she gave birth to to be named Jesus?

4. “The virgin will conceive and give birth to a son, and they will call him Immanuel.” Which Old Testament book contains this prophecy, and what does Immanuel mean?

5. The wise men from the east came to Jerusalem, and what did they come to do?

6. Where was the Messiah to be born, and which Old Testament book prophesied it?

7. Where did Joseph take Jesus and Mary to escape from Herod, and which Old Testament book contains the prophecy fulfilled by this?

8. Where did Herod have all the boys killed, and how old were they?

9. Whose words did this fulfill?

10. Why was Jesus called a Nazarene?

11. Who preached in the wilderness of Judea?

12. What did he say, and which prophet and book of the Old Testament prophesied about him?

13. As Jesus came up out of the water after being baptized, what was opened?

14. What did He see descending and alighting on Him?

15. What did a voice from heaven say?

---

Matthew 4-6

1. When the devil tempted Jesus, which book of the Bible did he quote from, and which book did Jesus quote from in response?

2. Jesus left Nazareth and went and lived in Capernaum, by the lake in the area of Zebulun and Naphtali. Whose words did this fulfill?

3. How many “blessed are” statements are there in Matthew 5:3–12?

4. What should also be shine?

5. What may they see?

6. Whom should they glorify?

7. Did Jesus come to abolish the Law and the Prophets, or to fulfill them?

8. Will anyone who is angry with a brother or sister be subject to judgment?

9. What has already happened in the heart of anyone who looks at a woman lustfully?

10. Anyone who divorces his wife, except for what reason, makes her the victim of adultery?

11. What does anyone who marries a divorced woman commit?

12. All you need to say is simply ‘Yes’ or ‘No’; anything beyond this comes from whom?

13. Whom should you not resist?

14. Who causes His sun to rise on the evil and the good and sends rain on the righteous and the unrighteous?

15. How should you pray?

16. What is your heart with?

17. Can you serve both God and money?

18. What was Solomon in all his splendor not dressed like?

19. What should you seek first? All these things will be given to you as well.

---

Matthew 7-9

1. What should not be given to dogs, and what should not be thrown to pigs, lest they trample them and then turn and tear you to pieces?

2. In everything, why should you do to others as you would have them do to you?

3. Is the gate that leads to life wide or narrow?

4. Is the road broad or narrow?

5. Are there many or few who find it?

6. Whom should you watch out for?

7. They come to you in sheep’s clothing, but inwardly are what?

8. Not everyone who says to Jesus, “Lord, Lord,” will enter the kingdom of heaven; who alone will enter it?

9. Why did Jesus say, “Truly I tell you, I have not found anyone in Israel with such great faith”?

10. Many who were demon-possessed were brought to Jesus, and He drove out the spirits with a word and healed all the sick. Which prophet’s words did this fulfill?

11. What do foxes have?

12. What do birds have?

13. But what does the Son of Man not have?

14. Which book does “I desire mercy, not sacrifice” come from?

15. Did Jesus Christ come to call the righteous or sinners?

16. What miracles did Jesus perform in Matthew 7—9?

---

Matthew 10-12

1. You have received freely; how should you give?

2. Jesus sent His disciples out like sheep among what?

3. What were they to be as shrewd as?

4. What were they to be as innocent as?

5. The disciples would be hated by everyone because of Jesus’ name, but what kind of person will be saved?

6. Whom should they not fear, and whom should they fear instead?

7. What will whoever finds their life lose?

8. What will whoever loses their life for Jesus’ sake find?

9. In Matthew 11:2–6, what exactly did John want to ask, and what did Jesus’ answer include?

10. “If you are willing to accept it, he is the Elijah who was to come.” Who is this person?

11. How should Matthew 11:16–17 be understood?

12. Which towns did Jesus rebuke in Matthew 11:20–24, and why?

13. When Jesus’ disciples were hungry on the Sabbath and began to pick heads of grain and eat them, did they sin?

14. When Jesus healed the man with the shriveled hand on the Sabbath, did He break the Sabbath?

15. Jesus healed all who were ill among those who followed Him and warned them not to tell others about Him. The words of which prophet did this fulfill?

16. If I drive out demons by the Spirit of God, then what has come upon you?

17. Anyone who speaks against the Son of Man can be forgiven, but anyone who speaks against whom will not be forgiven, either in this age or in the age to come?

18. Why will people have to give account for every empty word they have spoken on the day of judgment?

19. Jonah was three days and three nights in the belly of a huge fish; where will the Son of Man be for three days and three nights?

20. Whose brother,sister and mother is anyone who does the will of my Father in heaven?

---

Matthew 13-15

1. How many parables did Jesus tell in Matthew 13, and which prophecies were fulfilled?

2. When you read Matthew 14:13–21, where five loaves and two fish fed five thousand people, and Matthew 15:32–39, where seven loaves and a few small fish fed four thousand, which miracle in the Old Testament does this remind you of?

3. When the disciples saw Jesus walking on the lake, what did they feel and say?

4. What did Jesus say to them?

5. What did Peter want to do, and what happened when he did it??

6. What did Jesus say?

7. What did those in the boat say as they worshiped Jesus?

8. Which book contains the prophecy quoted in Matthew 15:8–9?

9. Why does what goes into a person’s mouth not defile them, but what comes out of their mouth does?

10. In Matthew 15:28, why did Jesus say, “Woman, you have great faith”?

---

Matthew 16-18

1. The Pharisees and Sadducees knew how to interpret the appearance of the sky, but what could they not discern?

2. Who do you say Jesus is?

3. Where must Jesus go, from whom must He suffer many things, and be killed, and on what day will He be raised to life?

4. Who will come in His Father’s glory with whom, and what will He do at that time?

5. What happened to Jesus before Peter and the others?

6. His face shone like what, and His clothes became as white as what?

7. Suddenly, who appeared to them and talked with Jesus?

8. While they were speaking, what suddenly covered them, and what did a voice say?

9. Who is the greatest in the kingdom of heaven?

10. Whom must you be careful not to despise?

11. Who in heaven always sees the face of Jesus’ Father?

12. Who also does not want any of these little ones to perish?

13. For where two or three gather in Jesus’ name, who is there with them?

14. How did Jesus answer Peter’s question, “Lord, how many times shall I forgive my brother or sister who sins against me? Up to seven times?”

15. If you do not forgive your brother or sister from your heart, who will also treat you that way?

---

Matthew 19-21

1. “That is why a man leaves his father and mother and is united to his wife, and the two become one flesh.” Which book is this from?

2. Husband and wife are no longer two, but what?

3. Therefore, what must no one do to what God has joined together?

4. According to the Law, why did Moses command a man to give his wife a certificate of divorce and send her away?

5. What good thing must one do to receive eternal life?

6. It is easier for a camel to go through the eye of a needle than for whom to enter the kingdom of God?

7. “With man this is impossible, but with God all things are possible.” What do we learn from Matthew 20:1–16?

8. Whoever wants to become first must become what?

9. Just as the Son of Man came not to do what, but to do what, and how did He come to serve?

10. What did Jesus feel for them, so that He touched their eyes and they immediately received their sight and followed Him?

11. “Say to Daughter Zion, See, your king comes to you, gentle and riding on a donkey, and on a colt, the foal of a donkey.” Which book contains this prophecy?

12. “My house will be called a house of prayer, but you are making it “a den of robbers.” Which book is this from?

13. “From the lips of children and infants you, Lord, have called forth your praise.” Where does this come from?

14. “The stone the builders rejected has become the cornerstone; the Lord has done this, and it is marvelous in our eyes.” Which book is this from?

---

Matthew 22-24

1. What kind of people are many, and what kind are few?

2. Is it lawful to pay taxes to Caesar?

3. At the resurrection, will people marry or be given in marriage?

4. Which commandment is the greatest in the Law?

5. If the Christ is the Son of David, how can David, speaking by the Spirit, call Him “Lord”?

6. How many times does Matthew 23 mention “woe”?

7. Jesus said, “From now on you will not see me until you say what?”

8. Why must you be careful that no one deceives you?

9. What things must happen, but the end is still to come?

10. What is the beginning of birth pains?

11. What must be preached in the whole world as a testimony to all nations before the end will come?

12. Whom should you flee from when you see standing in the holy place?

13. For at that time there will be what kind of trouble, unlike anything since the beginning of the world and never to be equaled again?

14. When will the sign of the Son of Man appear in heaven, and all the peoples of the earth mourn?

15. When will they see the Son of Man coming on the clouds of heaven, with power and great glory?

---

Matthew 25-28

1. The foolish virgins took their lamps but did not prepare what, while the wise took their lamps and prepared what in their jars?

2. Why must you keep watch?

3. To whom will more be given, so that they will have an abundance, and from whom will even what they have be taken away?

4. Whatever you did for whom, you did for the King?

5. Wherever this gospel is preached throughout the world, what will also be told in memory of her?

6. The Son of Man will go just as it is written about Him; but who is doomed to woe, and for whom would it have been better not to have been born?

7. When Jesus broke the bread and gave it to the disciples, what did He say it was?

8. Then He took the cup and gave it to them; what did He say?

9. Which book contains “I will strike the shepherd, and the sheep will be scattered”?

10. In Gethsemane, Jesus was deeply sorrowful and troubled, almost to what point?

11. What did He tell His disciples to do?

12. How many times did He pray, what did He say, and what were the disciples doing when He returned to them?

13. Although many came forward to give false testimony against Jesus, what could they not find?

14. Later, whom would they see sitting at the right hand of the Mighty One and coming on the clouds of heaven?

15. Why did Peter weep bitterly when the rooster crowed?

16. How much money did Judas receive for betraying Jesus?

17. How did Judas die, and what was the money used to buy?

18. What did Pilate do with water?

19. What did he say, and how did the crowd answer?

20. What prophecies were fulfilled by the gall in Matthew 27:34, the vinegar in verse 48, the casting lots for His clothes in verse 35, His crucifixion with robbers in verse 38, God forsaking Him in verse 46, and His burial place in verse 60?

21. Which Old Testament verse did the chief priests, teachers of the law, and elders quote when they mocked Jesus?

22. What charge was written on the sign above Jesus’ head?

23. What did the centurion and those guarding Jesus see that made them terrified, and what did they say?

24. What did the angel tell the women who came to look at the tomb?

25. In Galilee, what did Jesus say to His disciples?
