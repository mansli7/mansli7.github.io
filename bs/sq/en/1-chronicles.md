1 Chronicles 1–4

---


1. Whose name does not appear in 1 Chronicles, but is included in his brother’s name, and who is he?

2. Israel had two commanders who were related to King David. Who were they?

3. After Solomon, how many kings of Judah were there?

4. How many did what was right in the eyes of the LORD, and how many did what was evil?

5. Who was more honored than his brothers, and what did he cry out to the LORD, and was it granted?

---


1 Chronicles 5–8

---


1. Who was the firstborn of Israel?

2. Which tribe’s genealogy is not recorded in 1 Chronicles?

3. Which tribes were the first to be taken into exile Assyria?

4. To which tribe did Joshua belong?

5. Do you remember that the earth swallowed Korah’s followers, but Korah’s sons were not swallowed?

6. Who was a descendant of Korah and also a judge?

---


1 Chronicles 9–12

---


1. Was 1 Chronicles written before or after the exile of the people of Judah?

2. Which tribe was assigned Jerusalem, and which tribes lived there?

3. Which tribe’s members guarded the LORD’s temple gates?

4. Why did the LORD cause Saul to be killed?

5. Why did Joab become King David’s commander?

6. Before David became king, which tribes’ warriors followed him?

---


1 Chronicles 13–16

---


1. From the time the ark of the LORD was taken to its relocation to Jerusalem, where did the Ark stay?

2. David transported the Ark twice; how much time was between the two attempts, and why did the first one fail?

3. Who should all the earth sing to, whose salvation should be proclaimed daily, who created the heavens, and who will judge the whole earth?


---


1 Chronicles 17–20

---


1. After David was settled in his palace, what did he say to the prophet Nathan?

2. From where did the LORD take David, and what did He appoint him to be?

3. David set up garrisons in Edom, and the Edomites submitted to him. Do you recall the prophecy concerning Jacob and Esau?

4. These three were sons of the mighty men of Gath, and they died at the hands of David and his servants. Do you remember the bad report of ten of the twelve spies?

5. Is there a striking contrast?

---


1 Chronicles 21–24

---


1. God was displeased with the census of the people, and disaster came upon Israel. What did David pray to God?

2. Accepting and choosing the disaster God brings is also a lesson. Did David choose correctly?

3. Where did David build an altar for the LORD, what sacrifices did he offer, and whom did he call upon?

4. Why could David not build the LORD’s temple, but Solomon could?

5. Were the Levites more or less numerous than before entering Canaan?

6. How many clan leaders were there among the descendants of Aaron, and how many priestly divisions?

---


1 Chronicles 25–29

---


1. How many divisions of musicians were there to praise the LORD in the temple, and how many people in each division?

2. What were the descendants of Moses responsible for?

3. Did David finish numbering the people?

4. Who said, “I have in writing as a result of the Lord’s hand on me, and he enabled me to understand all the details of the plan”?

5. How much gold, silver, bronze, and iron did the Israelites dedicate for the use of the temple?

6. What does David say our days on earth are like?
