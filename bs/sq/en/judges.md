Judges 1-4

---


Did the tribe of Benjamin drive out the Jebusites living in Jerusalem?

What does Bokim mean, and why was it called that?

After Joshua and his generation died, did the later generations know the LORD?

Why did the LORD no longer drive out the remaining nations after Joshua’s death?

Who defeated Cushan-Rishathaim?

What was the name of the Moabite king whom Ehud killed?

Which woman did the LORD deliver Sisera into the hands of?

---


Judges 5-8

---


Who was with Gideon so that he would strike down the Midianites as one man?

What does “Jehovah‑Shalom” mean, and what is its origin?

(This is the KJV name; it may differ in the NIV.)

How many men did Gideon lead to defeat the vast army of the Midianites, the Amalekites, and the eastern peoples?

What did Gideon make and set up, and how did it become a snare to Gideon and his family?

---


Judges 9-12

---


Who killed his seventy brothers, and was his evil repaid?

Why did the LORD’s anger burn against Israel, so that He sold them into the hands of the Philistines and the Ammonites?

Do you think the vow Jephthah made to the LORD was good?

Why did the Ephraimites threaten to burn down Jephthah’s house over his head, and do you think they were right?


---


Judges 13-16

---


What name did the angel of the LORD give to Manoah?

What does the riddle “Out of the eater, something to eat; out of the strong, something sweet” mean, and how did the Philistines learn the answer?

How many Philistines did Samson kill with the jawbone of a donkey?

What is the origin of the spring En Hakkore?

Where did Samson’s strength come from?

Did Samson kill more people at his death than during his lifetime?

---


Judges 17-21

---


Micah had a shrine, and he made an ephod and some household gods and installed one of his sons as his priest. Was this in accordance with the LORD’s law?

According to the law, could Jonathan, the grandson of Moses, serve as a priest?

What do Judges 17, 18, 19, and 21 repeatedly say that Israel did not have?

The wicked acts of the men of Gibeah remind you of which city?

Was Benjamin right to refuse to hand over the wicked men of Gibeah to be put to death?

What did Israel not have in those days; everyone did as they saw fit?
