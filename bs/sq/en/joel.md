Joel 1-3

1. What kinds of insects invaded the land of Israel, destroying the grain, laying waste the fields, and causing the land to mourn?

2. Which trees withered and which trees faded away?

3. What moans, and what mills about?

4. Who are called to despair, wail, mourn, declare a holy fast, and cry out to the LORD?

5. Whose day is near?

6. On that day, before what comes, the land is like the garden of Eden; after it has passed, what does the land become?

7. Whose day is great and dreadful?

8. Is there anyone who can endure it?

9. Who quoted Joel 2:28—32 in the New Testament?

10. How many times does this book mention the day of the LORD, and are you looking forward to that day?
