# Joshua Study Questions

Joshua 1–4

---


1. What did the LORD refer to Moses?

2. What did the LORD command Joshua to meditate on day and night, and why?

3. Did the people of Jericho know that the LORD brought Israel out of Egypt and across the Red Sea, and were they afraid?

4. Who saved the two spies of Israel?

5. When crossing the Jordan River, what were the priests carrying, and did they cross by boat?

6. What was the meaning of the twelve stones set up on the west side of the Jordan River?

7. In which year, month, and day after the Exodus did the Israelites come up from the Jordan River and camp at Gilgal?

---


Joshua 5–8

---


1. What does the name Gilgal mean?

2. In which year, month, and day after the Exodus did the manna from heaven cease?

3. Were the walls of Jericho brought down by cannons or by explosives?

4. If not, how did the walls collapse?

5. After Jericho was captured, whose household alone was spared?

6. Why were the men of Ai able to kill thirty-six Israelites?

7. Why were the Israelites able, a few days later, to destroy all the people of Ai?

8. On Mount Ebal, what did the Israelites do according to what Moses the servant of the LORD had commanded?

---


Joshua 9–12

---


1. Why were the Israelites deceived by the Hivites of Gibeon and made a treaty with them?

2. Was the day on which Israel struck down the Amorites at Gibeon longer than other days?

3. Which were more in number: those killed by the LORD with great hailstones, or those killed by Israel with the sword?

4. Who fought for Israel?

5. Did Joshua fail to carry out anything that the LORD had commanded Moses?

6. How many kings did Joshua and the Israelites defeat west of the Jordan River?

---


Joshua 13–16

---


1. Which tribes settled east of the Jordan River?

2. In which year after entering Canaan did Eleazar and Joshua allot the land of Canaan to the Israelites?

3. Who received Hebron as an inheritance, and why?

4. Which tribe received land in the far south?

5. Where did the Canaanites live whom the tribe of Ephraim failed to drive out?

---


Joshua 17–20

---


1. Where was the Tent of Meeting set up?

2. Which tribe was located between Judah and the descendants of Joseph?

3. Which tribe’s inheritance was located within the territory of Judah?

4. What were the names of the six cities of refuge?

---


Joshua 21–24

---


1. How many cities did the Levitical priests receive, and within the territories of which tribes were they located?

2. Did any of the good promises the LORD made to Israel fail to come true?

3. Who said that which two tribes would be divided and scattered within the house of Jacob?

4. What was the name of the altar built by the two and a half tribes east of the Jordan, and what did it mean?

5. Who sent the hornet to drive out the two Amorite kings before Israel?

6. For what purpose did Joshua set up a large stone under the oak near the sanctuary of the LORD at Shechem?
