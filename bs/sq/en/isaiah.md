Isaiah 1-4

1. During the reigns of which kings of Judah did Isaiah son of Amoz receive the vision concerning Judah and Jerusalem?

2. Which New Testament book quotes Isaiah 1:9, “Unless the Lord Almighty had left us some survivors,we would have become like Sodom,we would have been like Gomorrah”?

3. When will the mountain of the LORD’s temple be established as the highest of the mountains and be exalted above the hills, and will all nations stream to it …?

4. When will the eyes of the arrogant be humbled, human proud be brought low, and the LORD alone be exalted …?

5. When will people throw awya the gold and silver idols, made for worship, to the moles and bats …?

6. When will the Lord snatch away their finery: the bangles and headbands and crescent necklaces, …?

7. When will seven women take hold of one man, saying, “We will eat our own food and provide our own clothes; only let us be called by your name, take away our disgrace”?

8. When will the Branch of the LORD be beautiful and glorious, and the fruit of the land be the pride and glory of the survivors in Israel…?

---

Isaiah 5-8

1. What is the vineyard of the LORD Almighty?

2. When Isaiah saw the Lord and His glory, why did he say, “Woe to me! I am ruined,” and how was his guilt taken away and his sin atoned for?

3. Who in the New Testament quotes Isaiah 6:9–10, “Be ever hearing, but never understanding; be ever seeing, but never perceiving. Make the heart of this people calloused; make their ears dull and close their eyes. Otherwise they might see with their eyes, hear with their ears, understand with their hearts, and turn and be healed”?

4. Isaiah 7:14, “The virgin will conceive and give birth to a son, and will call him Immanuel”, when was this fulfiled?

---

Isaiah 9-12

1. Isaiah 9:2, “The people walking in darkness have seen a great light; on those living in the land of deep darkness a light has dawned.”, when was this fulfilled?

2. Isaiah 9:6–7, who is the child born for us and the son given to us?

3. Whom does the LORD send against a godless nation, commanding him to seize loot and snatch plunder and to trample them down like mud in the streets?

4. Why does the Lord say, “I will punish the king of Assyria for the willful pride of his heart and the haughty look in his eyes”?

5. Who is the “he” in Isaiah 11:1–5?

---

Isaiah 13-16

1. Whose day is coming—a cruel day, with wrath and fierce anger, to make the land desolate and destroy its sinners within it?

2. Who will make the heavens tremble and the earth shake from its place in the day of his burning anger?

3. The pride and glory of which nation will be like Sodom and Gomorrah overthrown by God?

4. Isaiah 14:13–15, “You said in your heart: ‘I will ascend to the heavens; I will raise my throne above the stars of God; I will sit enthroned on the mount of assembly, on the utmost heights of Mount Zaphon; I will ascend above the tops of the clouds; I will make myself like the Most High’. But you are brought down to realm of the dead, to the depths of the pit,” who is the “you”?

5. Who appears at her high place, she only wears herself out; when she goes to her shrine to pray, it is to no avail?

---

Isaiah 17-20

1. Where will there no longer be the fortified city, and where will royal power vanish; the remnant of which nation will be like the glory of Israelities?

2. Who declares this?

3. What will be a sign and witness to the LORD Almighty in the land of Egypt?

4. Which country will the LORD strike, yet strike and heal, so that its people turn to the LORD and their prayers are answered?

5. On that day, Israel will be the third, along with which two nations, a blessing on the earth?

6. The Lord said: my servant Isaiah has gone stripped and barefoot for three years as a sign and protent against which two nations?

7. Likewise, the king of Assyria will carry away people from which nation, young and old, naked and exposed, bringing shame to which nation?

---

Isaiah 21-24

1. The watchman said: What has fallen, has fallen, and All the images of its gods lie shattered on the ground!?

2. On that day, when the LORD makes people weep and wail, tear out their hair and put on sackcloth, did the people obey?

3. Why will the ships of Tarshish wail?

4. On that day, whom will the Lord punish in the heavens and on the earth?

5. At that time, why will the moon be dismayed and the sun ashamed?

---

Isaiah 25-28

1. What will the LORD GOD swallow up forever; what will He wipe away from all faces; what will He remove from all the earth?

2. On that day, the LORD will punish Leviathan with His fierce, great and powerful sword, what does Leviathan represent here?

3. What does the Lord lay in Zion for a foundation?

4. Has it been tested?

5. Is it firm?

6. Is it a precious cornerstone?

7. Will the one who trusts in it panic?

---

Isaiah 29-32

1. The Lord has brought over you a deep sleep, sealed your eyes and covered your heads. What do “eyes” and “heads” refer to here?

2. Who quotes Isaiah 29:13 in the New Testament?

3. Why does the LORD say that Israel adds sin to sin?

4. Woe to those who go down to Egypt for help. What do they rely on instead of looking to Israel’s Holy One or seeking help from the LORD?

5. Egyptians are merely humans, not gods; their horses are flesh, not spirit. When one reaches out, those helping will stumble, and those helped will fall, all perishing together?

6. Who stretches out His hand so that both the helper and the helped fall together, perish together?

---

Isaiah 33-36

1. To whom do we cry for grace?

2. For whom do we long?

3. Whom do we ask to be our strength every morning and our salvation in times of trouble?

4. Who is our Judge, our Lawgiver, our King, and our Savior?

5. What will be dissilved?

6. What will be rolled up like a scroll?

7. What will wither like leaves from a vine or fig tree?

8. Whose sword is filled with blood, anointed with fat of lambs, goats’ blood, and the kidneys of rams; why is it prepared this way?

9. Whose sword is bathed in blood,and covered with fat — the blood of lambs and goats,fat from the kidneys of rams,and why?

10. Which streams will be turned into pitch,her dust into burning sulfur;her land will become blazing pitch!It will not be quenched night or day;its smoke will rise forever.From generation to generation it will lie desolate;no one will ever pass through it again?

11. “At that time the eyes of the blind shall be opened, the ears of the deaf unstopped; the lame shall leap like a deer, and the tongue of the mute sing.” Where is this fulfilled in the New Testament?

12. “The eyes of the blind be opened and the ears of the deaf unstopped;the lame leap like a deer,and the mute tongue shout for joy.”, where is this fulfilled in the New Testament?

13. Where else is the account of Isaiah 36 recorded in the Bible?

Isaiah 37-40

1. To which other book and chapter in the Bible is Isaiah chapter 37 is nearly identical?

2. How old was Hezekiah when he became ill to death, what two things did he ask of the LORD that year?

3. Where in the New Testament is Isaiah 40:3–5 fulfilled: “A voice cries: ‘Prepare the way of the Lord in the wilderness; make straight in the desert a highway for our God… The glory of the Lord shall be revealed; all flesh shall see it together; for the mouth of the Lord has spoken’”?

4. Where is Isaiah 40:3-5 fulfilled in the New Testament?

---

Isaiah 41-44

1. Whom does the LORD call His friend, and whom does He call a worm?

2. Who is the servant in Isaiah 42:1–3, and in which New Testament book is this fulfilled?

3. Who is given as a covenant for the people and a light for the Gentiles?

4. Who does the LORD say is His witness?

5. Who is the first and the last, apart from Him there is no God?

6. Which verses in Isaiah show that idols and the firewood come from the same tree?

---

Isaiah 45-48

1. Where is Isaiah 44:28 fulfilled in the Bible concerning Cyrus?

2. How many times in these four chapters is it said “I am the LORD, and there is no other” and how many times is it phrased “I am, and there is none besides me”?

3. What is the name of our Redeemer?

---

Isaiah 49-52

1. What does “it is too small a thing” refer to?

2. Who quotes, “I will also make you a light for the Gentiles, that my salvation may reach to the ends of the earth”?

3. Which New Testament book quotes,“In the time of my favor I will answer you,and in the day of salvation I will help you”?

4. Where does “these from the land of Sinim” refer to?

5. Whose arm is not too short to redeem?

6. Whose arm will bring justice to the nations, and whom do the islands look to and in His arm do they put their hope?

7. Whose arm, Awake, awake?

8. Who will lay bare His holy arm in the sight of all nations so that all the ends of the earth will see the salvation of our God?

9. Whose feet are beautiful on the mountains, and where is this quoted in the New Testament?

---

Isaiah 53-56

1. Isaiah 53:1 “Who has believed our message and to whom has the arm of the Lord been revealed?”. In which New Testament book is this fulfilled?

2. Isaiah 53:4a “Surely he took up our pain and bore our suffering”. In which New Testament book is this fulfilled?

3. Who quotes “By his wounds we are healed” in the New Testament?

4. Who reads Isaiah in the New Testament: “He was led like a lamb to the slaughter, and as a sheep before its shearers is silent……”,and later did he learn whom it refers to and was he baptized?

5. Based on these verses above, who is the arm of the LORD?

6. Which New Testament book quotes Isaiah 54:1?

7. Who in the New Testament quotes “All your children will be taught by the LORD”?

8. Which New Testament book mentions “even the sure mercies of David”?

---

Isaiah 57-60

1. “The righteous are taken away to be spared from evil”, do you recall in 1 Kings which king’s house had only one person buried?

2. Why were the fasts of Israel not acceptable to the LORD?

3. Israel’s iniquities have separated them from their God, what were these iniquities?

4. What will no more be your light by day; what will no longer shine on you?

5. Who will be your everlasting light; who will be your glory?

---

Isaiah 61-64

1. In which New Testament book, who read Isaiah 61:1–2 in the synagogue and say, “Today this Scripture is fulfilled in your hearing”?

2. By what has the LORD sworn: “Never again will I give your grain as food for your enemies, and never again will foreigners drink the new wine for which you have toiled”?

3. Who will eat and praise the LORD; who will drink in the courts of His Sanctuary?

4. Who is this coming from Edom, from Bozrah, with his garments stained crimson?

5. Who is this, robed in splendor,striding forward in the greatness of his strength?

6. What has been the name of the LORD from ancient times?

7. All of us have become like one who is unclean; what does our righteousness act like; what do we all shrivel up like; what are our sins like sweep us away?

---

Isaiah 65-66

1. Isaiah 65:1, “I revealed myself to those who did not ask for me; I was found by those who did not seek me. To a nation that did not call on my name, I said, ‘Here am I, here am I”, who are the people to whom God said?

2. When will “The former things will not be remembered, nor will they come to mind”?

3. “Dust will be the sprpent’s food”. Where is this prophecy first found?

4. What kind of people does the LORD look on with favor, what kind of people does He choose harsh treatment for?

5. See, the LORD is coming with fire; what are His chariots like; what will He bring down his anger with; what will he bring down his rebuke with?

6. With what will the LORD excute judgement on all people, will many be slain by Him?
