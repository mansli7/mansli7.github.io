Mark 1-3

1. How did John “prepare the way for the Lord” and “make straight paths for him”?

2. Where was Jesus for forty days, being tempted by Satan?

3. When Jesus called the fishermen Simon and Andrew, what did He say, and how did they respond?

4. Who has authority on earth to forgive sins?

5. When Jesus was having dinner at Levi’s house, who was eating with Jesus and His disciples?

6. For whom was the Sabbath made?

7. Who was not made for the Sabbath?

8. Therefore, who is also Lord even of the Sabbath?

9. Jesus appointed twelve, that they might always be with Him and that He might send them out to do what, and give them authority to do what?

10. Who was so busy that they were not even able to eat?

---

Mark 4-6

1. In the parable of the sower, what does the seed represent, and how many different kinds of people are there?

2. In the region of the Gerasenes, what was the name of the impure spirit?

3. What did the spirits beg Jesus to do?

4. Did Jesus permit them?

5. What did the man who had formerly been demon-possessed beg Jesus to let him do?

6. What did Jesus tell him instead?

7. What did the people of the region of the Gerasenes beg Jesus to do?

8. In Jesus’ hometown, who did the people think He was, and why did they take offense at Him?

9. How were five thousand men fed with five loaves and two fish, with twelve basketfuls of leftovers?

---

Mark 7-9

1. Were the traditions of the elders part of the Law given through Moses?

2. “Be careful,” Jesus warned them. “Watch out for the yeast of the Pharisees and that of Herod.” What were they to beware of?

3. What miracles did Jesus perform in Mark 7—9, and what did He predict?

---

Mark 10-12

1. How did God make the woman from the man, so that the two became one, and how did He say that a man should be united with his wife, so that the two become one flesh?

2. As Jesus and His disciples were going up to Jerusalem, who would the Son of Man be handed over to?

3. What would they condemn Him for, and to whom would they hand Him over?

4. How would they mock Him, spit on Him, flog Him, and kill Him?

5. After how many days would He rise again?

6. God’s house was to be called a house of what, but what had the Israelites made it?

7. What did Jesus curse, and what happened to it so that it withered from the roots?

8. What kind of people say that there is no resurrection?

9. Why did Jesus say that the poor widow who put only two very small copper coins into the temple treasury had put in more than all the others?

---

Mark 13-16

1. When people take you and hand you over to the authorities, why should you not worry beforehand about what to say, but say whatever is given to you at that time?

2. You will be hated by everyone because of Jesus’ name, but who will be saved?

3. Learn this lesson from the fig tree: when its branches become tender and its leaves come out, you know what is near. In the same way, when you see these things happening, who should you know is near, right at the door?

4. You will always have whom with you, and you can help them whenever you want; but you will not always have whom?

5. Who said, “Even if all fall away, I will not”?

6. Who took Peter, James and John along with Him and became deeply distressed and troubled?

7. Why was Pilate amazed?

8. Who came forward?

9. What kind of council member was he, and what was he waiting for?

10. He went boldly to whom and asked for what?

11. Early on the first day of the week, when Jesus had risen, to whom did He appear first?

12. Later, when the eleven were eating, Jesus appeared to them and rebuked them for their lack of faith and their stubborn refusal to believe. Why?

13. After the Lord Jesus had spoken to them, where was He taken, and where did He sit?
