Ecclesiastes 1-4

1. Who is the Preacher?

2. The more wisdom, the more what; the more knowledge, the more what?

3. Why does the Preacher hate life and all his labor?

4. Is there a time for everything, and a season for every activity under heaven?

5. God has made everything beautiful in its time. What has He also set in the human heart?

6. Yet what can no one fathom?

7. After death, where does the human spirit go, and where does the spirit of the beast go?

8. Whom does the Preacher declare happier than the living?

9. And whom does he consider better than both?

10. What kind of youth is better than an old and foolish king who no longer knows how to heed a warning?

---

Ecclesiastes 5-8

1. Do not be quick with your mouth, and do not be hasty in your heart to utter anything before God; why?

2. Everyone comes naked from their mother’s womb; how will they depart?

3. Can they carry anything away from their labor?

4. What have I observed to be good?

5. A stillborn child is better off than what kind of person?

6. Extortion turns whom into a fool, and bribery corrupts what?

7. God has made the one as well as the other—what are the “one” and the “other”, and why?

8. God made mankind upright, but what have they gone in search of?

9. What do the wise claim to know, yet cannot really comprehend?

---

Ecclesiastes 9-12

1. What are fish caught by, birds trapped by, and people snared by?

2. Do they know when their time will come?

3. What gives perfume a bad smell?

4. What can outweigh wisdom and honor?

5. As you do not know the path of the wind or how the body is formed in a mother’s womb, so whose work can you not understand?

6. The dust returns to the earth, and the spirit returns to whom?

7. Now all has been heard; here is the conclusion: what is the duty of all mankind?
