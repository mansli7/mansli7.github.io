Jeremiah 1-4

1. During the reigns of which kings of Judah did the word of the LORD come to Jeremiah?

2. How many kings were there in total?

3. When did the Lord know Jeremiah, set him apart, and appoint him as a prophet to the nations?

4. Why did Jeremiah not know how to speark;and how did the Lord respond?

5. What did Jeremiah see on two separate occasions?

6. What are the two sins Israel has committed, and what makes it evil and bitter for them?

7. If a man divorces his wife and she leaves him and marries another man,should he return to her again?

8. Though Israel has committed adultery with many lovers, can she still return to the LORD?

9. Whom will the LORD give to Israel to shepherd them with knowledge and understanding?

---

Jeremiah 5-8

1. In Israel, do the poor and foolish know the way of the LORD and the requirements of their God?

2. Do the leaders know?

3. From where will the LORD bring a nation against Israel?

4. Is it a mighty nation?

5. is it an ancient nation?

6. How have the Israelites forsaken the Lord and served foreign gods in the land of Israel, and where will they serve whom likewise?

7. As the Israelites have forsaken the LORD and served foreign gods in their own land, where and whom would they serve in return?

8. As a well pours out its water, so what dose Jerusalem pour out?

9. The Lord will act toward this temple, which is called by His name, and the land given to them and their ancestors, as He did previously where?

10. What is the name of the place to which the LORD did exactly what He will now do to the house that bears His Name—the temple the Israelites trust in, the place He gave to them and their ancestors?

11. The LORD told Jeremiah not pray for Israel nor offer any plea or petition for them; not plead with Him; why?

12. The stork in the sky knows her appointed seasons, and the dove, the swift and the thrush observe the time of their migration, but what do the people of Israel not know?

---

Jeremiah 9-12

1. What should the wise not boast of?

2. What should the strong not boast of?

3. What should the rich not boast of?

4. But what should the one who boasts boast about?

5. Whom should he have the understanding to know, and what should he know about Him?

6. In Jeremiah chapter 10, who is described as senseless and without knowledge?

7. Judah, have as many gods as you have towns; how many towns does Judah have?

8. Who has forsaken His house, abandoned His inheritance, and given the one He loves into the hands of her enemies?

---

Jeremiah 13-16

1. Whom does the linen belt represent?

2. Israel hoped for light, but the LORD would turn it into deep darkness and change it to deep gloom; what should they do to prevent this from happening?

3. Prophecy 1: “You will not see the sword or suffer famine. Indeed, I will give you lasting peace in this place”; Prophecy 2: “Do not pray for the well-being of this people. Although they fast, I will not listen to their cry; though they offer burnt offerings and grain offerings, I will not accept them. Instead, I will destroy them with the sword, famine and plague”. Which of these two prophecies comes from the LORD?

4. What four kinds of destroyers did the LORD appoint against Israel, and where would He make them abhorrent to all the kingdoms of the earth, and why?

5. Why did the LORD command Jeremiah not to marry or have sons and daughters in that place?

---

Jeremiah 17-20

1. Who is cursed, and who is blessed?

2. What is deceitful above all things and beyond cure?

3. Who searches the heart and examine the mind, to reward each person according to their conduct and deeds deserve?

4. What does the LORD teach Israel through the illustration of the potter and the clay?

5. As the LORD’s prophet, how was Jeremiah treated when he proclaimed the LORD’s messages?

---

Jeremiah 21-24

1. “The LORD says: See, I am setting before you the way of life and the way of death”, What is the way of life, and what is the way of death?

2. How many kings of Judah are mentioned in Jeremiah 22?

3. The days are coming, whom will the LORD will raise up for David?

4. How will he reign, what will he do in the land, how will Judah and Israel fare in his days, and by what name will he be called?

5. What do the two baskets of figs—one very good, one very bad—symbolize?

---

Jeremiah 25-28

1. For how many years will the Israelites serve the king of Babylon?

2. By that day, what will lie from one end of the earth to the other?

3. What will not be done for them, and what will they become?

4. Who prophesied against the LORD’s temple and Jerusalem, and should this person die? Why?

5. What would be taken to Babylon and remain there until the day the LORD came to them?

6. Then what would the LORD bring back andb restore to this place?

7. Who told Jeremiah to make a yoke out of straps and crossbars and put it on his neck?

8. What did it symbolize?

9. Who broke the yoke, and what happened as a result?

---

Jeremiah 29-32

1. What did the LORD command the exiles in Babylon to build and live in, to plant and eat, and to do with their sons and daughters so that they would increase and not decrease?

2. After how many years would the LORD come to them and fulfill His good promise to bring them back to this place?

3. Concerning those who were not carried into exile, what did the LORD Almighty prophesy, and why?

4. How did the LORD punish the false prophets Ahab, Zedekiah, and Shemaiah?

5. Jeremiah 31:15 says, “A voice is heard in Ramah, mourning and great weeping, Rachel weeping for her children…” When was this prophecy fulfilled?

6. Which New Testament book quotes Jeremiah 31:31–34 concerning the new covenant?

7. Why was Jeremiah confined in the courtyard of the guard in the royal palace of the Judah?

8. What did the LORD prophesy through Jeremiah’s purchase of a field?

---

Jeremiah 33-36

1. If the Israelites could break the covenant that the LORD made concerning what, causing what not to follow in their proper order at the appointed time, then could the covenant that the LORD made with whom be broken?

2. What freedom did the LORD proclaim for the people of Israel?

3. The LORD Almighty, the God of Israel, said that Jonadab son of Rekab would never fail to have a descendant serving before Him; why?

4. Baruch wrote on a scroll all the words the LORD spoke through Jeremiah. Which king of Judah burned the scroll, how would the LORD punish him?

---

Jeremiah 37-40

1. How many places was Jeremiah imprisoned, where, and why was he moved from one place to another, and who ultimately released him?

2. In which year and month did King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon besiege Jerusalem, and when was the city broken through?

3. Who pronounced sentence on the King Zedekiah of Judah and where was he taken?

4. Who was appointed governor over the towns of Judah?

---

Jeremiah 41-44

1. Who killed Gedaliah, the governor of Judah?

2. What did Johanan and the others ask Jeremiah to pray to the LORD for?

3. Did the LORD tell them to stay in Judah or go to Egypt, and did they obey?

4. Which land was Nebuchadnezzar going to attack, and what would happen to those destined for death, captivity, and the sword?

5. Where would the LORD punish the people of Judah who went to Egypt, and what sign would show them that the LORD’s words against them would stand?

---

Jeremiah 45-48

1. Who said: “I will overthrow what I have built and uproot what I have planted, throughout the earth”?

2. Where did they exclaim: Who is only a loud noise; he has missed his opportunity?

3. Who will be put to shame and given into the hands of the people of the north?

4. The Philistines heard the sound of the hooves of galloping steeds, the noise of enemy chariots, and the rumble of their wheels; fathers’ hands hung limp, and they did not turn back to look after their children. Why? Whom will Moab be ashamed of, just as Israel was ashamed for whom before? Of whom will Moab be ashamed, just as Israel was ashamed when they trusted in whom?”

5. Whose horn is cut off, whose arm is broken, and who declared this?

6. Who will be destroyed as a nation, because she defied whom?

---

Jeremiah 49-52

1. Who previously drove Israel out of her land, but now Israel will drive them out?

2. The Lord swears by himself that which place will become a ruin and a curse,[b] an object of horror and reproach; and all its towns will be in ruins forever?

3. Who will set fire to the walls of Damascus and consume the fortresses of Ben-Hadad?

4. Which place will become a haunt of jackals, a desolate place forever, where no one will live and no people will dwell?

5. Who will bring against Elam the four winds from the four quarters of heaven and scatter them to the four winds, so that there will not be a nation where the exiles do not go?

6. Who are the scattered sheep, driven out by lions; which king first devoured them, and which king later broke their bones?

7. Whom will the Lord Almighty, the God of Israel, punish along with his land, just as he punished which king before?

8. Who will bring Israel back to their own pasture, so that they will graze on Carmel and Bashan and their appetite will be satisfied on the hills of Ephraim and Gilead?

9. Who says: “In those days, at that time, search will be made for Israel’s guilt, but there will be none, and for the sins of Judah, but none will be found, for I will forgive the remnant I spare”?

10. Who was a gold cup in the Lord’s hand, making the whole earth drunk, whose wine the nations drank and therefore went mad?

11. Whom does the Lord purpose to destroy, and the spirits of which kings has he stirred up, because this is the vengeance of the Lord, vengeance for his temple?

12. Which place will become a heap of ruins, a haunt of jackals, an object of horror and scorn, a place where no one lives?

13. Here is the number of the people Nebuchadnezzar carried into exile: in his seventh year, how many Jews did he carry away; in Nebuchadnezzar’s eighteenth year, how many people from Jerusalem; in his twenty-third year, how many Jews were taken into exile by Nebuzaradan the commander of the imperial guard; and how many people were there in all?
