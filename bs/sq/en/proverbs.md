Proverbs 1-4

1. The fear of the LORD is the beginning of what; fools despise what?

2. What does the LORD give; what come from His mouth?

3. What does He store up for the upright; what is He to the blameless; whose course does He guard; whose way does He protect?

4. What will save you from the adulteress, from the wayward woman with her seductive words?

5. Whose ways will you walk in, and whose paths will you keep?

6. What should never leave you; bind them around your neck, write them on the tablet of your heart?

7. Whom should you trust with all your heart; do not rely on your own understanding, do not consider yourself wise; whom should you fear, and what should you shun?

8. My son, what should you not despise, and what should you not resent; why?

9. Gaining wisdom is better than gaining what; more profitable than what, more precious than what; what cannot compare with it?

---

Proverbs 5-8

1. In whom may you rejoice, like a loving doe, a graceful deer; may her breasts satisfy you always, may you ever be intoxicated with her love?

2. There are six things the Lord hates, seven that are detestable to Him; what are they?

3. Who can be had for a loaf of bread, and who preys on your very life?

4. Whom did the young man follow all at once, like an ox going to the slaughter, like a deer stepping into a noose till an arrow pierces his liver, like a bird darting into a snare, little knowing it will cost him his life?

5. In Proverbs 8:22–31, who is the “I” or “me”?

---

Proverbs 9-12

1. The fear of whom is the beginning of wisdom?

2. Knowledge of whom is understanding?

3. If you are wise, who will benefit?

4. If you are a mocker, who will suffer alone?

5. What stirs up conflict?

6. What covers over all wrongs?

7. A beautiful woman without discretion is like a gold ring in what animal’s snout?

8. What is a wife of noble character to her husband?

9. A disgraceful wife is like what in her husband’s bones?

---

Proverbs 13-16

1. Does the person who spares the rod love their children?

2. What does a wise woman build?

3. What does a foolish woman tear down?

4. Who believes anything?

5. Who gives thoughts to his steps?

6. The fear of whom is a fountain of life, turning a person from what?

7. Whose eyes are everywhere, keeping watch on the wicked and the good?

8. Better a little with the fear of the LORD than what?

9. Better a small serving of vegetables with love than what?

10. Whom is wisdom’s instruction to fear, and humility comes before honor?

11. To humans belong the plans of the heart, but from whom comes the proper answer of the tongue?

12. All a person’s ways seem pure to them, but who weighs the motives?

13. The LORD works out everything to its proper end – even the wicked for what kind of day?

14. The lot is cast into the lap, but its every decision is from whom?

---

Proverbs 17-20

1. The crucible for silver and the furnace for gold, but what does the LORD test?

2. Children’s children are a crown to the aged, and what are parents to their children?

3. Better to meet a bear robbed of her cubs than what kind of person?

4. A brother wronged is more unyielding than what; disputes are like what?

5. Who is a father’s ruin?

6. What is like the constant dripping of a leaky roof?

7. What are inherited from parents, but who is from the LORD?

8. Whoever is kind to the poor lends to whom; who will reward them for what they have done?

9. What is a mocker; what is a brawler; whoever is led astray by them is not wise?

---

Proverbs 21-24

1. Better to live on a corner of the roof than share a house with whom?

2. All day long he craves for more, but who gives without sparing?

3. Start children off on which way, and even when they are old they will not turn from it?

4. What kind of person should you not make friends with or associate with, or you may learn their ways and get yourself ensnared?

5. Do not withhold discipline from a child; if you punish them with the rod, what will not happen to them?

6. Punish them with the rod and save them from what?

7. The father of what kind of child has great joy; the father of what kind of son rejoices in him?

8. Eat honey, my son, why is it good?

9. Know what is like honey to your soul?

---

Proverbs 25-28

1. Who can be persuaded through patience?

2. What can be broken by a gentle tongue?

3. Seldom set foot in whose house, lest they hate you?

4. Which New Testament book cites Proverbs 25:21–22?

5. Why should you not answer a fool according to his folly?

6. Why should you answer a fool according to his folly?

7. The crucible for silver and the furnace for gold – but what tests man?

8. What kind of person will lack nothing?

9. What kind of person will receive many curses?

---

Proverbs 29-31

1. Two things I ask of you, Lord; do not refuse me before I die: what are they?

2. There are three things that are too amazing for me, four that I do not understand: what are they?

3. What kind of woman is described in Proverbs 31?
