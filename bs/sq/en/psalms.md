Psalms 1–8

What kind of person is blessed?
Where is Psalm 2:1–2 quoted in the New Testament, and who is the Anointed One here?
Where is “You are my Son; today I have begotten you” quoted in the New Testament, and how is it understood?
Where is Psalm 8:4–8 quoted in the New Testament, and who is “man”?

---

Psalms 9–17

What kind of person trusts in the LORD, and why?
What kind of people go down to hell?
Who is in His holy temple? Who is on His heavenly throne? Whose eyes observe everyone on earth? Who examines the righteous?
What kind of person says in his heart: “There is no God”?
Whom will the LORD not let see decay?
Are there people whose reward is only in this life?

---

Psalms 18–23

Where else in the Bible does the content of Psalm 18 appear?
What refreshes the soul, gives wisdom to the simple, brings joy to the heart, gives light to the eyes, endures forever, and is altogether righteous?
Psalm 22 is a prophecy concerning whom, and where is it fulfilled in the New Testament?
Who is my shepherd, so that I shall not be in want?

---

Psalms 24–32

Where is Psalm 24:1 quoted in the New Testament, and regarding what matter?
Who is the King of glory?
In Psalm 25, what kind of people are put to shame, and what kind are not?
The LORD is my what, so that I shall fear no one?
One thing I ask from the Lord, and this only I do seek, what is it?
Whose anger lasts only a moment, but whose favor lasts a lifetime?
Who said: “Into your hands I commit my spirit” in the New Testament?
In Psalm 32, what kind of person is blessed?

---

Psalms 33–37

By whose word were the heavens made, and by whose breath all their starry hosts?
Does the LORD observe all people and consider everything they do?
Which New Testament book quotes Psalm 34:12–16?
Psalm 34:20 is fulfilled in whom in the New Testament, and can the bones of the Passover lamb be broken?
Where is Psalm 35:8 “may ruin overtake them by surprise” quoted in the New Testament?
Where is Psalm 36:1 quoted in the New Testament?
In Psalm 37, who shall inherit the land?

---

Psalms 38–41

In what condition did David ask the LORD not rebuke or discipline him?
I will watch my ways, lest I sin with what?
Are we foreigners and strangers before the LORD?
Psalm 40:6–7 speaks of whom, and is it fulfilled?
Who quotes Psalm 41:9 in the New Testament?

---

Psalms 42–47

My soul longs for the LORD, as the deer pants for what?
In Psalms 42 and 43, how does the psalmist describe his soul?
The Israelites did not gain the land by their own sword or might, but by what?
Who is our refuge and strength, an ever-present help in trouble?

---

Psalms 48–54

Mount Zion — whose city is it, where is it lofty and beautiful, for whom is it a joy, and what does it reveal itself to be?
Can those who trust in their wealth and boast of their riches redeem the life of another or give to God a ransom for them, so that they may live on forever and not see decay? Why or why not?
Who will redeem my soul from the power of the realm of the dead?
When you see others grow rich and their houses increase in splendor, should you fear? Why?
People who have wealth without understanding are like what?
God does not delight in sacrifices or burnt offerings; what offering does God desire?
Which psalm is very similar to Psalm 53?

---

Psalms 55–60

In Psalm 56, how many verses are there, and how many times is trust in God mentioned?
In whose shadow shall I take refuge?
From whom are the wicked estranged from birth, and what do they do from the womb?
With whom will we gain the victory, and why?

---

Psalms 61–65

My soul finds rest in whom, and from whom comes my salvation?
Who alone is my rock, salvation, fortress, so that I will never be shaken?
The lowborn are but a breath; the highborn are but a lie; where are they placed? Together, they are lighter than what?
In a dry, parched and waterless land, for whom do I thirst?
Who will rejoice in the LORD and take refuge in Him?

---

Psalms 66–72

O God, You tested us, refined us like what?
In Psalm 68:18, “You ascended on high… ” — who is “You,” and where is this quoted in the New Testament; who else does “You” refer to?
“ The Lord daily bears our burdens,” who is the Lord here?
Who enables a person to escape death?
Psalm 69 references — who is fulfilled in?
Who quoted Psalm 69:9 in the New Testament?
Who is fulfilled by Psalm 69:21 and Psalm 69:22–23?
In old age, what do I ask the LORD; in my weakened strength, what do I ask?

---

Psalms 72–75

What is around the necks of the wicked and the arrogant, and what clothes them?
Who from ancient times has been my King, bringing salvation on earth?
Who set all the boundaries of the earth; who made both summer and winter?
Who brings one down and exalts another?
Who holds the cup full of foaming wine mixed with spices; when it is poured out, who drink it to the dregs?

Psalms 73–75

What is around the necks of the wicked and the arrogant, and what clothes them?
Who from ancient times has been my King, bringing salvation on earth?
Who set all the boundaries of the earth; who made both summer and winter?
Who brings one down and exalts another?
Who holds the cup full of foaming wine mixed with spices; when it is poured out, who drink it to the dregs?

---

Psalms 76–79

Where is God known, where His name is great, where is His tabernacle, and where is His dwelling place?
Psalm 78:2 “I will open my mouth with a parable; I will utter hidden things, things from of old” — who in the New Testament Gospels spoke in parables?
Psalm 78 recounts past events—list three events and their Scripture references.

---

Psalms 80–84

In Psalm 80, how many verses contain “Restore us, O God; make your face shine on us, that we may be saved”?
Who have fed Israel with the bread of tears; who have made them drink tears by the bowful?
Who in the New Testament quotes Psalm 82:6 “I said, you are gods”?
The LORD’s enemies conspire together — who are these enemies?
Psalm 84 says, what kind of person is blessed?

---

Psalms 85–89

What meet together, what kiss each other, what springs from the earth, and what looks down from heaven?
The LORD’s love toward me is great; from where has He delivered my soul?
Psalm 86:15 “You, Lord, are a compassionate and gracious God, slow to anger, abounding in love and faithfulness” — what is its source?
Where does the LORD love more than all the other dwellings of Jacob?
With whom did the LORD make a covenant, swearing to whom: “I will establish your line forever and make your throne firm through all generations”?

---

Psalms 90–95

Who is like the new grass of the morning, that springs up new in the morning, but by evening is dry and withered?
How many years is the span of our life; if strong, how many years; yet what is our boast, quickly passing?
Which verses in Psalm 91 did the devil use to tempt Jesus Christ; how did he twist God’s Word, and how did our Lord resist?
How great are your works, Lord, how profound your thoughts!
Psalm 94 — what kind of person is blessed?
Where is Psalm 95:11 quoted in the New Testament?

---

Psalms 96–100

Where has Psalm 96 previously appeared in the Bible?
What surrounds the LORD; what is the foundation of His throne?
Whose right hand and holy arm have worked salvation and revealed His righteousness to the nations?
We are His creation and belong to Him; we are His people and the sheep of His pasture — how should that shape our response?

---

Psalms 101–106

What kind of people may dwell with me, and what kind of people must not dwell in my house?
Who looks down from His sanctuary on high to hear the groans of the prisoners and to release those condemned to death?
Psalm 104 — which Pentateuch book corresponds to which parts?
Psalm 105:1–15 previously appeared in which book?
Psalm 105:16–45 records events from Genesis and Exodus — list two events and their references.

---

Psalms 107–110

Psalm 107, how many times does it say “They cried out to the LORD in their trouble, and He delivered/saved/brought them from/out of their distress”?
Psalm 108 previously appeared in which book and which chapter?
Psalm 109:8 “may another take his place of leadership” — in which wicked person is this fulfilled?
Who in the New Testament quotes Psalm 110:1?
Psalm 110:4 — who is this fulfilled in?

---

Psalms 111–118

What is the beginning of wisdom; who are those who have good understanding; who is to be praised forever?
In Psalms 111–113 and 115–117, whom should you praise?
Who made the idols? Can they speak with mouths, see with eyes, hear with ears, smell with noses, feel with hands, walk with feet, or utter a sound with throats?
In the New Testament, who quotes Psalm 118:22–23 and 118:26?

---

Psalms 119

What has the LORD commanded us, that we should diligently keep?
I seek the LORD with all my heart; do not let me stray from what?
May the LORD open what, that I may see the wonderful things in His law?
May the LORD keep me from what, and graciously grant me what?
What do I love so much that I meditate on it all day long?
What is a lamp for my feet and a light to my path?

---

Psalms 120–126

In my distress, on whom do I call, and He answers me?
From where does my help come?
In whose name is our help?
What are those who trust in the LORD like?
When the LORD brought back the captives to Zion, what were we like?

---

Psalms 127–132

Psalm 127 has only five verses; how many times does it say “in vain”?
What is the inheritance from the LORD, and what is His reward?
What kind of person is blessed, enjoys prosperity, is favored, and receives the LORD’s blessing?
To whom did The LORD swear an oath to, a sure oath He will not revoke: “One of your own descendants I will place on your throne”?
Which place did the LORD choose and desire as His dwelling, saying: “This is my resting place for ever and ever; here I will sit enthroned, for I have desired it”?

---

Psalms 133–136

“What a good and pleasant thing it is when God’s people live together in unity!” - What is this compared to? And what else is it compared to?
In Psalm 136, how many verses are there, and how many times does it say “His love endures forever”?

---

Psalms 137–140

Though the LORD is exalted, whom does He look on kindly, and whom does He see from afar?
Who created my inmost being; who knit me together in my mother’s womb?
Whom do I praise, for I am fearfully and wonderfully made? Whose works are wonderful, which I know full well?
I was formed in darkness, intricately woven in the depths of the earth; at that time, my frame was not hidden from whom? When I was made in the secret place and woven together in the depths of the earth, from whom was my frame not hidden?
Whose eyes saw my unformed body; and whose book contained all the days ordained for me before even one of them came to be?

---

Psalms 141–144

Do not let my heart be drawn to what, to take part in wicked deeds with what kind of people? I cry to you, LORD; what do I say; “my portion in the land of the living”? Who should hear my prayer, listen to my cry for mercy, and in His faithfulness and righteousness come to my relief? What are human beings like? What are their days like?

---

Psalms 145–148

What are the first and last verses of Psalms 146–148? Whom does the LORD uphold the cause of? Whom does He give food to? Whom does He set free? Whose eyes does He give sight to? Whom does He lift up? Whom does He love? Whom does He watch over? Whom does He sustain? Whose way does He frustrate? In Psalm 148, how many times does it say “Praise”?

---

Psalms 149–150

In these two psalms, what are the opening and closing words? Psalm 150—how many verses are there, and how many times does it say “Praise the LORD”, “Praise God” and “Praise Him”?
