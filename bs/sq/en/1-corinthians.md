1 Corinthians 1-3

1. Was Paul’s letter written only to the church in Corinth?

2. What divisions were there in the church in Corinth?

3. Where is the statement “I will destroy the wisdom of the wise; the intelligence of the intelligent I will frustrate” found?

4. What is Paul explaining by using it here?

5. What do the Jews demand, what do the Greeks look for, and whom do we preach?

6. What is Christ to the Jews and to the Gentiles?

7. But to those whom God has called, both Jews and Greeks, what is Christ to them?

8. Where is the statement “Let the one who boasts boast in the Lord” found?

9. What does it mean here?

10. What is the wisdom of God’s mystery that was hidden in the past?

11. Where is the statement “What no eye has seen, what no ear has heard, and what no human mind has conceived—the things God has prepared for those who love him” found?

12. How can we understand the deep things of God?

13. Who is God’s temple?

14. Who is its foundation, and whose Spirit dwells in it?

---

1 Corinthians 4-6

1. How should people regard Paul and his fellow workers—as servants of whom and stewards of whose mysteries?

2. What is required of stewards?

3. Paul and his fellow workers worked with their own hands. When they were cursed, how did they respond?

4. When they were persecuted, how did they respond?

5. When they were slandered, how did they respond?

6. What were they still regarded as?

7. Although you may have ten thousand guardians in Christ, why do you not have many fathers?

8. What kind of sexual immorality was found in the Corinthian church?

9. How was the man who committed it to be dealt with?

10. Paul had previously written to the Corinthian church not to associate with sexually immoral people. Which people was he referring to?

11. Who is our Passover lamb?

12. Who will judge the world and the angels?

13. What kind of people will not inherit the kingdom of God?

14. Where is the statement “The two will become one flesh” found?

15. What does it mean here?

---

1 Corinthians 7-9

1. If someone was a slave when called to follow the Lord, whose freed person are they?

2. If someone was free when called, whose slave are they?

3. The unmarried man is concerned about whose affairs and how he may please whom?

4. But the married man is concerned about what kind of affairs and how he may please whom?

5. Was Paul married?

6. From which verse do you know?

7. What makes people proud, and what builds them up?

8. If anyone loves God, who is that person known by?

9. What cannot make us acceptable to God? Why? But what should you be careful about, lest what happen?

10. Where is the statement “Do not muzzle an ox while it is treading out the grain” found?

11. What does it mean here?

12. What authority is Paul referring to when he says, “I have not made use of any of these rights”?

13. If I do not preach the gospel, what am I under?

14. If I willingly do this, what is my reward?

15. I strike a blow to my body and make it my slave, so that after preaching to others, I myself might not be what?

---

1 Corinthians 10-12

1. When the Israelites came out of Egypt, they were all under the cloud, passed through the sea, and were baptized into whom in the cloud and in the sea. They all ate the same spiritual food and drank the same spiritual drink. The rock that accompanied them was a spiritual rock, and who was that rock?

2. Where is the statement “The people sat down to eat and drink and got up to indulge in revelry” found?

3. What did the Israelites make at that time?

4. The temptations you face are common to what?

5. What will God, who is faithful, not let you be tempted beyond?

6. When you are tempted, what will He also provide?

7. In everything, seek to please everyone, not seeking whose good but whose good, so that who may be saved?

8. Whose head is Christ?

9. Whose head is man?

10. Whose head is God?

11. Who is the image and glory of God?

12. Who is the glory of man?

13. Was man created for woman, or was woman created for man?

14. Woman came from whom, and man was also born of woman, but all things come from whom?

15. Where are the statements “This is my body, which is for you; do this in remembrance of me” and “This cup is the new covenant in my blood; do this, whenever you drink it, in remembrance of me” found?

16. Who is the “I” referring to?

17. What ministries or roles has God appointed in the church?

---

1 Corinthians 13-16

1. According to 1 Corinthians 13, what is love?

2. What three things remain, and which of them is the greatest?

3. What builds up oneself, and what builds up the church?

4. Where are the statements “With other tongues and through the lips of foreigners I will speak to this people, but even then they will not listen to me” and “God is really among you” found?

5. What do these quotations help us understand here?

6. According to 1 Corinthians 15, what is the gospel?

7. In whom do all die, and in whom will all be made alive?

8. Where is the statement “For he ‘has put everything under his feet’” found?

9. Who is “he” referring to?

10. Where is the statement “Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die” found?

11. What kind of people are the “we” referred to here?

12. The first man Adam became a living being, and the last Adam a life-giving spirit. Who are these two “Adams”?

13. What is the mystery Paul reveals in 1 Corinthians 15?

14. Where is the statement “Death has been swallowed up in victory” found?

15. When will it be fulfilled?

16. Where are the statements “Where, O death, is your victory? Where, O death, is your sting?” found?

17. What is the sting of death?

18. What is the power of sin?

19. Through whom do we have victory?

20. At the end of 1 Corinthians, what does Paul pray will be with us?
