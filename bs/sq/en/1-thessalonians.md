1 Thessalonians 1-3

1. What did Paul and his companions continually remember before God our Father concerning the church in Thessalonica?

2. When the gospel came to you, was it simply with words, or also with what?

3. Since God had approved us as being entrusted with the gospel, we speak not to please whom, but to please whom?

4. “We wanted to come to you—certainly I, Paul, did, again and again—but who blocked our way?”

5. Where was Paul when he wrote this letter?

6. “So when we could stand it no longer, we thought it best to be left by ourselves in Athens. We sent who to find out about your faith?” What did Paul want to know?

7. What was he afraid of?

8. What did he fear might have been in vain?

---

1 Thessalonians 4-5

1. What is God’s will?

2. “According to the Lord’s word, we tell you this directly.” What is it?

3. The day of the Lord will come like what?

4. We should not be like others, who are asleep, but what should we do?

5. What should we put on as a breastplate, and what should we wear as a helmet?

6. Who did not appoint us to suffer wrath but to receive salvation through our Lord Jesus Christ?

7. What is God’s will for you in Christ Jesus?
