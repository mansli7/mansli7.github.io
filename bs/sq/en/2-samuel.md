# 2 Samuel Study Questions

2 Samuel 1–4

---


1. Saul had pursued David for many years. Now that Saul was dead, did David rejoice or mourn and weep?

2. Whether he could go up to Judah, and even which city to go to—whom did David inquire of?

3. Where was David anointed king over the house of Judah?

4. Why did Joab kill Abner?

5. Was it during battle, and was it ordered by King David?

6. Did the two leaders of raiding bands who killed Saul’s son Ish-bosheth receive a reward from David?

---


2 Samuel 5–8

---


1. How old was David when he became king, and how many years did he reign?

2. Could he attack the Philistines, and whom did David inquire of?

3. Why did the LORD strike Uzzah dead?

4. Did Saul’s daughter Michal bear children for David?

5. In “I will establish the throne of his kingdom forever”, who does “his” refer to?

6. In “I will be his father, and he shall be my son”, who does “he” refer to?

7. Who gave David victory wherever he went?

---


2 Samuel 9–12

---


1. Why did David show kindness to Mephibosheth?

2. Why did David summon Uriah back to Jerusalem?

3. Who killed Uriah?

4. Who despised the LORD’s command？

5. What evil did he do in the LORD’s sight？

6. Whom did he give opportunity to blaspheme because of it？

7. How will the evildoer be punished?

---


2 Samuel 13–16

---


1. Amnon disgraced Tamar, and Absalom killed Amnon—why did such calamity occur in David’s house?

2. According to the law, how should they have been punished?

3. Who arranged for Absalom to return from Geshur to Jerusalem?

4. Why did David flee Jerusalem, yet leave the Ark of God behind?

5. How did David respond to Shimei’s cursing?

6. How did Absalom treat David’s concubines left behind, and whose prophecy did this fulfill?

---


2 Samuel 17–20

---


1. Why did Absalom and others say that “The advice of Hushai the Arkite is better than that of Ahithophel”?

2. How did Ahithophel die?

3. Who killed Absalom?

4. Even after defeating Absalom and his followers, why did the whole army’s victory turn into mourning?

5. Did David execute Shimei who cursed him?

6. Who divided the land with Sheba?

7. How did Joab kill Amasa?

8. How was the rebel Sheba killed?

---


2 Samuel 21–24

---


1. Why was there a famine for three successive years during David’s reign?

2. In which book of the Bible did Israel swear not to destroy the Gibeonites?

3. In David’s psalm, who is his fortress, rock, stronghold, shield, horn of salvation, high tower, refuge, light in darkness, and strength?

4. In 2 Samuel 23:3–7, whose Spirit spoke through David?

5. Who made an everlasting covenant with the house of David?

6. Why did David refuse to drink the water three mighty warriors drew from the well by the gate of Bethlehem?

7. Why did David conduct a census of the people of Israel and Judah?

8. When faced with the three judgments the LORD would bring, how did David choose?
