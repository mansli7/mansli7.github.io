Luke 1-3

1. Who will be great in the sight of the Lord, drink no wine or other fermented drink, and be filled with what even before birth?

2. Whom will he turn back to whom?

3. He will go on before the Lord in whose spirit and power, to turn whose hearts toward their children and the disobedient to what kind of wisdom, and to make ready what for the Lord?

4. Why did Zechariah become unable to speak?

5. The virgin Mary would conceive and give birth to a son. What was he to be called?

6. He would be great and be called what?

7. Whose throne would the Lord God give him?

8. He would reign over whose descendants, and for how long?

9. What would never end?

10. Why would the holy one to be born to the virgin Mary be called the Son of God?

11. Where did Joseph and Mary live before going to Bethlehem?

12. Why did they go to Bethlehem, and where was Jesus born?

13. On the day Jesus was born, who announced to the shepherds what good news of great joy?

14. Who joined him in praising God, and what did they say?

15. Where did the shepherds hurry to, whom did they find, what did they spread the word about, and whom did they glorify and praise?

16. There was a man in Jerusalem named what?

17. He was righteous and devout and was waiting for what, and what was on him?

18. What had been revealed to him, so that he would not die before seeing whom?

19. Moved by what, he entered the temple and met whom?

20. Whom did he take in his arms, and what did he say in praise of God?

21. Whose wisdom and stature, and what kind of favor, continued to increase?

22. How is Jesus’ genealogy in Luke different from the one in Matthew?

23. …

Luke 4-6

1. On the Sabbath, in the synagogue in Nazareth, Jesus read from Isaiah 61:1–2 and said, “Today this scripture is fulfilled in your hearing.” How did the people respond?

2. Was their response right?

3. During the three and a half years when the sky was shut and there was a severe famine throughout the land, where was Elijah sent to a widow?

4. The man who was healed of leprosy was from which country?

5. Where are these two events recorded in the Old Testament?

6. What did Simon Peter see that caused him to fall at Jesus’ knees and say, “Go away from me, Lord; I am a sinful man!”?

7. “But to you who are listening I say: Love whom, do good to them, and lend to them without expecting anything back. Then your reward will be great, and you will be sons of whom? Why? And be merciful, just as whom is merciful?”

8. “Give, and it will be given to you.” What will be given to you, and why will it be poured into your lap with a good measure, pressed down, shaken together and running over?

9. Why does a good person bring good things out of the good stored up in the heart, while an evil person brings evil things out of the evil stored up in the heart?

10. …

Luke 7-9

1. As Jesus approached the town gate of Nain, what did He encounter?

2. Whom did He see, whom did He have compassion on, what did He say to her, and what did He do for her?

3. At the house of Simon the Pharisee, what did a sinful woman do to Jesus, and what did Jesus say to her?

4. As Jesus traveled from one town and village to another, proclaiming the good news of the kingdom of God, who went with Him, and who supported Jesus and His disciples out of their own means?

5. A woman who had been subject to bleeding—how many years had she suffered, how much had she spent on doctors, and could any doctor heal her?

6. How was her bleeding stopped?

7. As the time approached for Jesus to be taken up to heaven, He resolutely set out for Jerusalem. Passing through Samaria, why did the people there not welcome Him?

8. When His disciples James and John saw this, what did they say, and how did Jesus rebuke them?

9. …

Luke 10-12

1. The seventy returned with joy. What were they rejoicing about, and what did Jesus tell them to rejoice about instead?

2. What kind of people were the priest and the Levite, and what kind of people lived in Samaria?

3. Who was the neighbor of the man who fell into the hands of robbers?

4. In Luke 11:24, “I will return to the house I left.” What does “the house” refer to?

5. What is so inexpensive that two of them are sold for two pennies, yet God does not forget a single one of them?

6. What else has even been numbered?

7. Do not be afraid; you are worth more than what?

8. Why must you be on your guard and be careful to guard yourselves against every kind of greed?

9. …

Luke 13-15

1. Yeast can be used as a picture of something good, such as what, and also something bad, such as what?

2. Which city often killed the prophets and stoned those sent to it?

3. From now on, the people of Israel will not see whom until they say what?

4. Everyone who exalts themselves will be humbled, and those who are what will be exalted?

5. Did any of the people whom the owner of the great banquet had invited beforehand come to the banquet?

6. What kind of people came instead?

7. Briefly describe the key turning points in the younger son’s life and what happened at each one.

8. …

Luke 16-18

1. The rich man was suffering in Hades. What did he need to relieve his suffering?

2. When he saw Abraham far away, could he cross over to Abraham?

3. If people do not listen to Moses and the Prophets, will they be persuaded even if someone rises from the dead?

4. As Jesus traveled along the border between Samaria and Galilee, how many people with leprosy did He cleanse?

5. How many returned to thank Jesus and give praise to God, and what kind of person was he?

6. What similarities are there between the days of the Son of Man and the days of Noah and Lot?

7. Of the Pharisee and the tax collector who prayed in the temple, which one was justified?

8. …

Luke 19-21

1. Who was Zacchaeus?

2. Whom did he want to see, and why?

3. Why did he climb a sycamore-fig tree?

4. With what kind of attitude did he welcome Jesus into his home that day?

5. What did he say he would give to the poor, and how many times would he repay anyone he had cheated?

6. Who said that salvation had come to his house?

7. How did Jesus answer the spies’ question, “Is it right for us to pay taxes to Caesar?”

8. God is not the God of whom, but the God of whom? Why?

9. There will be great distress in what place, and wrath against whom?

10. Who will fall by the sword and be taken as prisoners to all nations?

11. What will be trampled on by the Gentiles, and until when?

12. What signs will appear before the coming of the Son of Man?

13. How should you always do what, and continually do what, so that you may be able to escape all that is about to happen and stand before whom?

14. …

Luke 22-24

1. Satan entered the heart of whom?

2. He was one of the Twelve. What did he go to discuss with the chief priests and the officers of the temple guard?

3. Who wanted to sift you as wheat?

4. But who had prayed for you, so that your faith would not fail?

5. And when you had turned back, whom were you to strengthen?

6. An angel from heaven appeared and gave strength to whom?

7. Who was in anguish and prayed more earnestly, with His sweat like drops of blood falling to the ground?

8. Who was very glad when he saw Jesus, and why?

9. Why did he then treat Jesus with contempt and mock Him?

10. Who had previously been enemies of each other, but became friends with each other on that day?

11. Did Pilate and Herod find any guilt in Jesus?

12. How should we understand, “If people do these things when the tree is green, what will happen when it is dry”?

13. To whom did Jesus say, “Truly I tell you, today you will be with me in paradise,” and why?

14. Jesus Himself came up and walked along with them, but what kept them from recognizing Him?

15. Jesus took bread, gave thanks, broke it and began to give it to them. What was opened, enabling them to recognize Him?

16. When Jesus appeared to His disciples after His resurrection, did He have flesh and bones?

17. Then what did Jesus open so that they could understand the Scriptures?
