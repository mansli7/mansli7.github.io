# ℹ️ About This Site

---

## 🎯 Purpose

This site was built to encourage people to read God's Word and understand salvation through Jesus Christ — the **Last Adam** (1 Corinthians 15:45).

> For as in Adam all die, even so in Christ shall all be made alive.
> — 1 Corinthians 15:22

The name **末後的亞當 (The Last Adam)** refers to Jesus Christ, who became the life-giving spirit. Where the first Adam brought death through disobedience, the Last Adam brings eternal life through His obedience and resurrection.

---

## 📖 Bible Reading Plans

The annual reading plan guides you through the entire Bible in one year.

- **6 reading days per week**, covering Old and New Testament books
- **1 review day** per week to reflect and consolidate
- Available in **English** and **Traditional Chinese (繁體中文)**
- **Excel download** for offline use and printing

Consistent, daily reading transforms your understanding of Scripture and deepens your relationship with God.

---

## ❓ Study Questions

For each book of the Bible, guided study questions are provided to help you:

- **Observe** — What does the text say?
- **Interpret** — What does it mean in context?
- **Apply** — How does it relate to your daily life?
- **Connect** — How does it fit into the broader story of Scripture?
- **Respond** — How should you respond to God?

Study questions are available in both English and Chinese.

## 💻 Technology

The Tech section shares programming tools and scripts — including Python code for generating Bible reading calendars, so anyone can create their own customised plan.
