## Design Philosophy

All three designs:
- ✅ Match your warm light theme
- ✅ Work perfectly on mobile
- ✅ Represent both Bible study and technology
- ✅ Are CSS-only (no image files needed)
- ✅ Have smooth hover animations
- ✅ Scale beautifully at all sizes

**Current choice (Design 1) is recommended for:**
- Professional appearance
- Modern tech aesthetic
- Strong brand identity
- Best balance of Bible + Tech themes
